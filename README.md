# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

  

## Objective

I wanted to investigate how coffee shops operate by gathering information on profits, the most popular items, and the number of orders over a specific period in order to identify interesting patterns

  

## Dataset

Source, period, what one row represents.
This dataset contains the sales data of a coffee shop. It contains the transaction id of every customer and their order description. It does not contain any null value.
It contains transaction id, transaction date, transaction time, store id as there are multiple stores, store location, product id, transaction quantity, unit price, total bill i.e. transaction quantity * unit price, product category and many others.
  

## Business Questions

1. What is the revenue generated by each product category?

2. What are the 3 most frequently ordered product types in each category?

3. What is the average bill amount?

4. What was the revenue generated in each month in 2023?

5. How do the results from each month compare to those from the previous month?

  

## Tools

SQL, DuckDB, Jupyter, VSCode

  

## Key Findings
- The two most frequently ordered items are gourmet brewed coffee and barista espresso.
- Total revenue for all coffee shops in this dataset has been rising significantly almost every month. Revenue in June was double that in January, indicating business growth.
- As the average total bill exceeds the calculated median, we can safely assume that significant proportion of orders are large.
3–5 short findings.
